Hello! 

I am a Predoctoral Research Fellow at Yale School of Management, where I am very fortunate to be advised by [Stefano Giglio](https://sites.google.com/view/stefanogiglio/?authuser=3) and [Johannes Stroebel](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~jstroebe/). My research interests include expectation formation, household finance, and climate finance. I graduated _cum laude_ from Columbia University with a joint degree in Economics-Statistics in May 2024. 
At Columbia, I conducted research under [Daniel Björkegren](https://dan.bjorkegren.com/) on 2 projects: evaluating the effectiveness of Generative AI in improving educational outcomes for underresourced communities and working with the transport authority of a developing country to build public transport infrastructure. 

Fun facts: I'm from the Philippines, I play the [euphonium]([url](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euphonium)), and I enjoy playing basketball and volleyball.

**I will be starting my PhD in Financial Economics at Yale this fall :)**
