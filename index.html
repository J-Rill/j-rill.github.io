I am a PhD student in Financial Economics at the Yale School of Management. I was also previously a Predoctoral Research Fellow at Yale SOM, where I was very fortunate to be advised by Stefano Giglio and Johannes Stroebel. My research interests include expectation formation, household finance, and climate finance. I graduated cum laude from Columbia University with a joint degree in Economics-Statistics in May 2024, and was an RA for Dan Björkegren.

Fun facts: I'm from the Philippines, I play the euphonium, and I enjoy playing basketball and volleyball.
