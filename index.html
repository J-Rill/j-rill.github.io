Joachim Rillo

Predoctoral Research Fellow · Yale School of Management

I am a Predoctoral Research Fellow at Yale School of Management, advised by Stefano Giglio and Johannes Stroebel. My research interests span expectation formation, household finance, and climate finance.

I graduated cum laude from Columbia University with a joint degree in Economics-Statistics in May 2024. At Columbia, I worked with Daniel Björkegren on the effectiveness of generative AI in improving educational outcomes for underresourced communities, and on public transport infrastructure in developing countries.

I will be starting my PhD in Financial Economics at Yale in Fall 2026.
